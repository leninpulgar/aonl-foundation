Our Mission

The AONL Foundation provides resources that create opportunities to bridge science and practice to shape the future of nursing leadership.

Our Vision

A healthier tomorrow, advanced through nursing leadership.

Who We Support

Nurse Leaders at Every Stage

Foundation-funded programs reach nurse leaders across every stage of their careers — from those just stepping into leadership to seasoned executives shaping care for entire health systems.

Nurse Executives CNOs and senior leaders setting strategy for their organizations.
Nurse Managers & Directors Leaders guiding day-to-day care delivery and team performance.
Emerging Nurse Leaders Nurses stepping into their first formal leadership roles.
Nurse Researchers Scholars advancing the evidence base behind nursing leadership.

Together, they're building a stronger, more resilient nursing workforce.
